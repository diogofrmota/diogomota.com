# New Apps Plan

**App name:** Beer / Wine Log
**App description:** Keep a personal journal of beers and wines you try.
**Features:** Log drinks with photos, producer, style, date and location; add ratings and tasting notes; filter history and save favourites.

**App name:** Expense Splitter
**App description:** Track shared expenses and settle up with friends.
**Features:** Create groups; record who paid; split equally or by custom amounts; show balances and suggested repayments; mark debts as settled.

**App name:** Habits
**App description:** Build routines and track consistency.
**Features:** Create daily or weekly habits; set targets and reminders; check off completions; view streaks and calendar history; pause or archive habits.

**App name:** Running Log
**App description:** Record runs and follow your progress.
**Features:** Log date, distance, duration, route and notes; calculate pace; track weekly and monthly totals; view personal bests and distance goals.

**App name:** Workouts
**App description:** Plan workouts and track strength training.
**Features:** Create routines; log exercises, sets, reps and weights; use a rest timer; review workout history and personal records.

**App name:** Subscriptions
**App description:** Track recurring payments and upcoming renewals.
**Features:** Save cost, billing cycle and renewal date; show monthly and yearly totals; send renewal reminders; track trials and cancellations.

**App name:** Mafia
**App description:** A social deduction game with hidden roles and day/night rounds.
**Features:** Create a room; assign private Mafia, town, detective and doctor roles; guide night actions and daytime discussion; vote to eliminate players; detect the winning team.

**App name:** Flags Quiz
**App description:** Test your knowledge of countries and their flags.
**Features:** Guess countries from flags; choose multiple-choice or typed answers; filter by continent; play timed rounds; track scores, accuracy and missed flags.

**App name:** Benfica
**App description:** Plan the days you need to go to the stadium for Benfica home matches.
**Features:** Add or update fixtures with opponent, competition and kickoff; mark attendance plans; view upcoming stadium days in a calendar; track ticket status; set match reminders and export calendar events.
